# DESIGN.md — METEK Editorial Cinema

Shipped visual contract for METEK Digital. The hero remains the cinematic signature; every surface after it behaves like an authored studio publication, not a SaaS component library.

## Thesis

The site opens as a film, then becomes a portfolio issue. Scale, real material, precise typography and deliberate negative space create the continuity. Decoration never substitutes for proof.

## Hero boundary

The glass M, film, refraction, atmosphere, framing and motion system are a protected composition. Redesign work after the hero must respond to its confidence without copying its effects.

## Post-hero grammar

- Monochrome page surfaces: Carbon, Mist and paper whites.
- Petrol is reserved for the intro/hero system and channel-specific states; it is not a decorative page accent.
- Large display type establishes chapters. Small mono type is limited to folios, metadata and colophon information.
- Asymmetric 12-column spreads replace equal card grids.
- Real project and studio media is shown large, often edge-to-edge or with intentional cropping.
- Hairlines, underlines and whitespace provide structure. No ornamental dots, glow or dashboard timelines.
- Captions live with their image; titles and core metadata remain in the same reading frame.

## Page rhythm

1. Hero film
2. Project index as staggered editorial plates
3. Featured case as a split visual essay
4. Capabilities as three image-led dossiers
5. Studio frames as physical evidence
6. Manifesto and approach as typographic folios
7. Notes as a magazine cover story and compact reading list
8. Contact as a direct colophon-style closing spread

## Secondary routes

- `PageHero` is a typographic masthead with a folio rule, not a reusable marketing banner.
- `/work` is a portfolio index built from raw project media and alternating spans.
- `/work/[slug]` is a case-study issue: cover, gallery, decisions, result and credits.
- `/services` is an expandable typographic ledger; hierarchy comes from scale and rules.
- `/approach` is a production folio, never a node-and-line timeline.
- `/manifesto` is a founder/studio spread followed by numbered positions.
- `/blog` has one cover story and an asymmetric article index; articles use a numbered magazine grid.
- Final CTAs and the footer are direct contact and colophon surfaces without promo cards.

## Type

- Display: Goks (`.font-display`)
- Accent face: Vireon (`.font-subtitle`), used sparingly
- UI and reading: Space Grotesk
- Display headlines use tight leading and tracking; body copy remains calm and comfortably readable.

## Motion

Motion reveals hierarchy and media; it does not decorate static UI. Preserve the orchestrated intro and hero behavior. Below the fold, use short mask/fade reveals and restrained image parallax. Reduced motion must remain fully legible.

## Do not

- Equal feature-card grids as page scaffolding
- Decorative petrol dots, connector lines, neon glow or radial washes
- Pill clusters and glass/chrome controls outside the hero
- Stock imagery that only illustrates “digital work”
- Repeated eyebrow/heading/body/button templates
- Device chrome when the project image itself can carry the composition
- Low-opacity body text that weakens reading confidence

## Finish

A page is finished only when its typography, media crop, mobile order, four-locale resilience, interaction states and browser visibility have been reviewed.
