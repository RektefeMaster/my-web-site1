# Audience segments — METEK

Do not write for a generic “KOBİ.” Pick one primary segment per surface (secondary optional).

## 1. `new_service_site`

**Who:** Service businesses that need a first serious website.  
**Job:** Look credible, explain offer, get inquiries.  
**Language:** Clear offer, trust cues, simple path to contact. Avoid enterprise jargon.

## 2. `rebuild_legacy`

**Who:** Established businesses stuck on an old or template site.  
**Job:** Replace friction and dated trust signals without losing what already works (SEO equity, phone leads).  
**Language:** Migration calm, continuity, concrete upgrades (speed, mobile, CMS).

## 3. `ops_panel`

**Who:** Companies that need a custom admin panel, CRM, or internal workflow UI.  
**Job:** Daily-use software — not a brochure.  
**Language:** Operators, data, actions, handoff; skip “digital transformation.”

## 4. `boutique_brand`

**Who:** Design-sensitive boutiques, fashion, jewelry, small hotels, editorial brands.  
**Job:** Taste, atmosphere, and conversion without template look.  
**Language:** Material, craft, restraint; still one clear CTA.

## 5. `global_vertical`

**Who:** US/global local-service and trade brands (remodeling, cabinetry, field service) plus portfolio-fit global fashion/boutique/jewelry/hotel.  
**Job:** Compete in their market’s SERP and sales motion (often quote-led or appointment-led).  
**Language:** Market-native terms (e.g. EN-US: cabinets, remodeling, get a quote) — never translated TR idioms.

## Home (golden)

Primary: mix of `boutique_brand` + `global_vertical` + `ops_panel` signaled lightly. Hero stays broad but proof-backed; capabilities section names the three lanes.

## Services (golden)

Map packages explicitly to segments 1–5 in the services brief.
