# Locale briefs

## en-US

- **Market:** United States (also default site locale)
- **Formality:** Plain professional American English
- **CTA habits:** “Start a project”, “Get a quote”, “View work”
- **Avoid:** Turkish sentence rhythm, British-only spelling in UI, translated idioms
- **Search research:** US SERP terms (cabinets, remodeling, custom website design, etc. per page)

## tr-TR

- **Market:** Türkiye
- **Formality:** Direct professional; “siz” in UI CTAs OK; founder “ben” on manifesto
- **CTA habits:** “Proje başlat”, WhatsApp common for SMBs
- **Avoid:** Calqued EN agency phrases (“brief”, “CTA” in UI), empty “dijital dönüşüm”
- **User-facing lexicon:** birincil eylem / çağrı (not CTA); proje notu / ihtiyaç özeti / fikir (not brief/brif). “Düğme” yalnızca UI kontrolü.
- **Search research:** Native TR queries only (see per-page `briefs/**/shared.md` → `## Market: tr-TR`)

## de-DE

- **Market:** Germany / DACH-facing
- **Formality:** Sie-form in UI; concrete and trustworthy; less hype than EN marketing
- **CTA habits:** “Projekt starten”, “Arbeit ansehen”
- **Length:** Expect longer compounds — compress aggressively for buttons/headlines
- **Avoid:** Anglicism spam; literal EN calques
- **Search research:** Native DE queries (Webdesign Agentur, individuelle Website, Admin-Panel…)

## es-US

- **Market:** United States Spanish (not es-ES, not Mexico-only slang dump)
- **Formality:** Clear professional usted/neutral; natural US Hispanic business web tone
- **CTA habits:** “Iniciar proyecto”, “Ver trabajos”
- **Avoid:** Spain-only lexicon where US differs; literal TR/EN translation cadence
- **Search research:** Queries actually used by es-US searchers in the relevant vertical
