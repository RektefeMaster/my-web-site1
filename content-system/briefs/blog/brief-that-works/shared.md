# Blog: brief-that-works — shared semantic brief

```yaml
page_goal: Help a buyer prepare the information needed to scope a website, panel, or chatbot project.
primary_audience: [new_service_site, rebuild_legacy, ops_panel, boutique_brand, global_vertical]
visitor_problem: References and feature wishes arrive without a business task, owner, content plan, or decision process.
main_message: A useful project note states the problem, users, required actions, available content, constraints, and decision owner.
proof_points: [single-accountability, content-review-before-publish]
required_information:
  - Business problem and primary user
  - Required actions and integrations
  - Content ownership and available assets
  - Timing, decision maker, and explicit exclusions
  - A compact example
desired_action: Prepare the project note or start discovery with METEK.
search_intent: Informational and commercial investigation.
tone_constraints: Practical and buyer-friendly; in Turkish never use “brief/brif” in user-facing copy.
claims_not_allowed: [guaranteed schedule reduction, fixed savings, universal template]
```
